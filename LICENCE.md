## 🧩 About

**PUBG Hack** is a comprehensive mod menu for PlayerUnknown's Battlegrounds featuring aimbot, ESP, wallhack, radar hack, no recoil, and item ESP.

Based on popular mods like **PixelBot**, **PUBG Tool**, and **FuryAim**.

## ✨ Features

### 🎮 Core Hacks
- Aimbot — automatic targeting and tracking
- ESP — see enemies through walls
- Wallhack — remove obstacles and view enemies
- Radar Hack — mini-map reveals all enemies
- No Recoil — remove weapon recoil

### 🎯 Aim & Accuracy
- Silent Aim — hit without crosshair alignment
- Trigger Bot — auto-fire when enemy crosses your sights
- Spread Control — tighten bullet spread

### 🗺️ Map & Vision
- Item ESP — highlight weapons and loot
- Vehicle ESP — locate vehicles
- Player Names — display player names and health
